## Full description
Jobsite Spanish helps English-speaking supervisors and tradespeople learn the Spanish they actually need on Texas construction sites.

387 cards across 13 trades: Safety, Tools, Framing, Concrete, MEP, Site work, Equipment, Measurements, Quality, Finishes, Weather, Office, and Phrases.

How it works:
• Study mode with Leitner spaced repetition — cards you miss come back sooner
• Listen mode for hands-free audio practice using your phone’s offline text-to-speech
• Browse the full deck by trade
• Track streak and progress on the Stats screen
• In-app Feedback to report bugs or ideas

Built for the jobsite:
• Works fully offline after install — no account, no ads, no network required while studying
• Progress stays on your device only
• Portrait, fast, and simple to use with gloves-off downtime

Made for construction supervisors and crew leads who want practical Spanish, not classroom fluff.

One-time purchase ($1.99). No ads, no subscription, no account.
