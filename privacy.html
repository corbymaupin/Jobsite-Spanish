Privacy Policy

Jobsite Spanish · last updated September 2026

Short version: this app collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Everything it does happens on your own device.

What data this app collects

None. Jobsite Spanish does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers of any kind.

What data this app stores, and where

Your study progress — which words you've learned, your review schedule, and your streak — is saved only in your device's local browser storage (localStorage). This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is never sent to any server, because the app has no server to send it to
  • is not visible to the developer or to anyone else
  • is deleted if you clear your browser/app data, and is not backed up anywhere by this app

Network access

The app makes no network requests while in use. Speech playback (Listen mode) uses your device's own built-in text-to-speech engine, entirely offline. The one-time page load to install or update the app is the only network activity involved, and that request carries no personal data — it's the same as loading any web page.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no third-party libraries that phone home.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.
