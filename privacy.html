Privacy Policy

Jobsite Spanish · last updated September 2026

Short version: study data stays on your device; the app has no backend servers. Optional feedback email leaves the device only if you choose to send it.

What data this app collects

None. Jobsite Spanish does not collect, transmit, or have access to any personal information, usage data, analytics, or identifiers of any kind.

What data this app stores, and where

Your study progress — which words you've learned, your review schedule, and your streak — is saved only in on-device app storage (Android private app storage / DataStore; the web demo uses browser localStorage). This data:

  • stays on your device during normal study use
  • is never uploaded to an app server — the app has none
  • is not visible to the developer unless you choose to contact us
  • is deleted if you clear the app's storage / data, and is not backed up by this app to our servers

Network access

Study, Listen, Browse, and Stats work fully offline. Speech playback uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The Play / web install or update is the only required network activity for getting the app, and that request carries no study data.

Optional feedback: if you use the in-app Feedback form and send email, the message (and optional reply address) leaves your device through your own email app to james.corby.maupin@gmail.com. Nothing is sent until you tap send in your mail client. You can also copy the feedback text without sending anything.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting, no third-party libraries that phone home.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, this applies equally to users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices ever change, this page will be updated to reflect that before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about this policy: james.corby.maupin@gmail.com
